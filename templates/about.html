About AttendEase

College Event Attendance Management System

Overview

AttendEase is a Django-based web application that streamlines the process of requesting and approving attendance for college events. It provides dedicated dashboards for three roles — Students, Coordinators, and Teachers — connected through a structured two-stage approval workflow.

Approval Workflow
Student Submits Coordinator Reviews Teacher Approves Attendance Marked

Open (external) events skip the coordinator step and go directly to the teacher.
